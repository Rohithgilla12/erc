## Design Context

### Users
Club runners and their coaches, equally. Runners glance at a run on their phone just after finishing, often outdoors in bright light or on the way home. Coaches read an athlete's week at a desk, comparing runs and writing feedback. Every run page has to work for a ten-second phone glance and a careful desk review.

### Brand Personality
Calm, coaching, encouraging. ERC is a volunteer running club, and the site should read like a coach's notebook: plain, readable, and honest about the numbers, with gentle emphasis on progress rather than raw performance theatre.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Light and dark themes both exist in `apps/web/src/app/(frontend)/styles.css`; keep both. Brand orange (`--color-accent`) is a fill under ink only, never text; text uses `--color-accent-text`.
- System font stack, tabular figures for every number.
- Anti-reference: a Strava clone. No orange-everything activity page, no copied Strava layout. Also avoid SaaS KPI card grids.
- Outdoor readability: key numbers large and high-contrast, values in full-strength ink rather than muted grey, generous tap targets.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with the three numbers a runner checks first (distance, time, pace); everything else is quieter and further down.
2. Ink first, colour as meaning. Lines and numbers are ink; colour marks effort (heart-rate zones) or a highlight (fastest split), and always has a text label too.
3. Ruled-notebook structure: hairlines and alignment instead of boxes and shadows. No cards inside cards.
4. Everything works without JavaScript and at 390 px wide; tables scroll inside themselves, never the page.
5. Motion only where it explains a change; respect reduced motion.
